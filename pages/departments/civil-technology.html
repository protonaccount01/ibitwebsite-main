Diploma in Civil Engineering

Diploma in Civil Engineering can pave the way for a promising career. Civil engineering, a vital branch of modern engineering and technology, primarily focuses on planning, design, and structural execution. This field encompasses a wide range of challenging projects, including supervising public works design and construction activities such as roads, bridges, tunnels, buildings, airports, dams, sewerage systems, ports, and more. A Civil Engineer plays a pivotal role in the planning, implementation, and maintenance of projects. Individuals with a Diploma in Civil Engineering possess both technical expertise and administrative skills necessary for the job. They can hold positions such as site engineer/manager, design consultant, researcher, educator, etc., across various construction projects in both public and private sectors. Moreover, they have opportunities to work as Deputy Assistant Engineers in government, semi-government, and autonomous organizations like LGED, PWD, R&H, BR, BBA, MES, EED, WASA, DESCO, DPDC, WDB, BADC, Bangladesh Airlines, Telecom Industry, as well as public and private housing and development projects.

Career Prospects

A Diploma in Civil Engineering opens up a wide range of career prospects in both public and private sectors. Graduates with this qualification can pursue various roles such as:

  • ✓
    Site Engineer/Manager: Responsible for overseeing construction activities on-site, ensuring compliance with design specifications, and managing project timelines and resources.
  • ✓
    Design Consultant: Involved in the planning and design phase of construction projects, including creating blueprints, structural analysis, and ensuring building code compliance.
  • ✓
    Deputy Assistant Engineer: Working in government, semi-government, or autonomous organizations in roles related to infrastructure development, urban planning, and public works projects.
  • ✓
    Construction Project Manager: Overseeing the entire construction project, including budgeting, scheduling, procurement, and coordination with various stakeholders.
  • ✓
    Quality Control Engineer: Ensuring the quality and safety of construction materials and processes through testing, inspection, and adherence to industry standards.
  • ✓
    Building Inspector: Inspecting buildings and structures to ensure compliance with building codes, zoning regulations, and safety standards.

Admission Eligibility

  • Minimum SSC equivalent from any discipline with minimum GPA: 2.00 .
  • HSC (Science) Students can directly join in 3rd Semester.
  • HSC (Vocational) students can directly join in 4th Semester. (Passing year 2011, GPA: 2.00)

Enrollment Requirements

To enroll in Headway Engineering Institute, you need to fulfill the following requirements:

  • Application Form: Collect the application form from the college campus desk and fill it out by hand.
  • SSC Certificate/Transcript: Provide the original certificate or transcript from the SSC or equivalent exam.
  • Photographs: Submit 3 color passport-sized photos and 2 stamp-sized photos.
