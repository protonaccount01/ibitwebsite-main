About Us

IBIT - Islami Bank Institute Of Technology, Sylhet, established in 1999, is a polytechnic institute operated by Islami Bank Foundation and approved by the Bangladesh Technical Education Board. Located on the 3rd, 4th, and 5th floors of Newa Corner at Humayun Rashid Chattar, Kadamtoli, Dakshin Surma, Sylhet-3100, the institute has made a significant impact in the region by providing quality engineering education to students. We offer 4-year diploma programs in Electrical Engineering, Civil Engineering, and Computer Science & Engineering. Our courses are thoughtfully designed to combine theoretical knowledge with practical application, ensuring students gain a comprehensive understanding of their chosen fields.

Our experienced faculty members are dedicated to mentoring students throughout their academic journey. We create a supportive and vibrant campus environment, offering recreational and educational trips alongside extracurricular activities to enrich students' overall learning experience. We focus on preparing students for successful careers by emphasizing professional development, industry connections, and social responsibility. Our strong ties with industry leaders provide students with valuable networking opportunities and potential career pathways.

At IBIT - Islami Bank Institute Of Technology, Sylhet, we are committed to empowering students to achieve their full potential and succeed in the engineering industry. We appreciate your consideration of us as your partner in education and in building your bright future.
